# Privacy policy

PlanBox is a personal planner that runs on your own devices.

- PlanBox has no servers of its own and collects no data.
- Your data stays in a database on your devices.
- If you turn on Google Drive sync, PlanBox stores one file per device in
  your Google Drive's hidden app data folder (scope `drive.appdata`). It
  cannot see or access any other files in your Drive.
- Your sign-in token stays on your device. You can revoke PlanBox's access
  at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
